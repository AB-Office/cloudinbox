Consolidate Multiple Emails into One

You can aggregate POP3-compatible emails such as custom domain emails, provider emails, and rental server emails into CloudInbox.

  • No need to switch accounts
  • Prevent missed emails
  • Simplified email management

Emails are Stored "Encrypted"

CloudInbox stores email bodies in encrypted form. Third parties cannot read the content.

Encryption and "Key Management" are Separate

CloudInbox manages the "keys" that decrypt the encryption using Google Cloud's dedicated services.

Cloud KMS

Securely store and manage encryption keys

  • Key rotation
  • Access control
  • Audit logs

Secret Manager

Securely store confidential information such as passwords

  • Not stored directly in code
  • Only accessible to necessary processes

Keys and passwords are never stored directly in applications or databases.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is it a complete replacement for Gmail?
A. CloudInbox is an alternative to "External Email Import (POP3 aggregation)".
Q. Is POP3 secure?
A. After reception, emails are encrypted and stored securely.
Q. Where are passwords stored?
A. They are securely managed in Secret Manager.
Q. Is there a smartphone app?
A. Android/iOS apps are scheduled to be available by the end of March 2025. Currently, you can use the Web app (PC and smartphone browser compatible).
Q. Are there any notes about sending emails?
A. Email sending may not be available due to IP restrictions on the mail server. Please add the allowed IP to your mail server, or use other email clients to send emails.
