# Pricing / Billing

Last Updated: January 3, 2026

## Plans

CloudInbox offers two plans to meet your needs.

### Free Plan

- **Price**: Free
- **Storage**: 2GB
- **Email Accounts**: 1 account
- **Features**:
  - Automatic email reception
  - Cloud mailbox
  - Encrypted storage
  - PC and smartphone support

### Standard Plan

- **Price**: $5/month (tax included)
- **Storage**: 50GB
- **Email Accounts**: Multiple accounts
- **Features**:
  - Automatic email reception
  - Cloud mailbox
  - Encrypted storage
  - PC and smartphone support
  - Ad-free

※ The Standard plan is scheduled to be available by the end of February 2026.

## Payment Methods

The Service is contracted on the web. Payment method details will be displayed on the Service when it becomes available.

## Billing

- Billing is done on a monthly basis.
- Payment is processed automatically each month.
- The billing cycle starts from the date of subscription.

## Plan Changes

- You can upgrade from the Free plan to the Standard plan at any time.
- When upgrading, the new plan will take effect immediately.
- When downgrading from the Standard plan to the Free plan, the change will take effect at the end of the current billing period.

## Cancellation and Refunds

- You can cancel your subscription at any time.
- Cancellation will take effect at the end of the current billing period.
- Refunds for usage fees are not provided in principle, except as otherwise determined by AB-Office.
- For details, please refer to the [Terms of Service](/terms.html).

## Contact

For inquiries regarding pricing and billing, please contact us through the [Contact Form](/contact.html) or by email at ab.office.jp@gmail.com.
